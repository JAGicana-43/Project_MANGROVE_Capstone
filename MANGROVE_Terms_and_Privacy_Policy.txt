2. DESCRIPTION OF THE SYSTEM

Project M.A.N.G.R.O.V.E is a GIS-based spatial decision-support platform
that assists in selecting soil sampling sites for mangrove rehabilitation.
The System:

  - Lets users define an Area of Interest (AOI) and generate drone survey
    mission files;
  - Analyzes uploaded orthomosaic imagery using a pretrained image
    classification model to produce land cover and suitability scores;
  - Generates GPS-guided soil sampling points;
  - Pairs with an Android mobile application and an IoT soil probe to
    collect, geotag, and synchronize pH, electrical conductivity, moisture,
    and NPK readings;
  - Visualizes results through dashboards, heatmaps, and downloadable
    reports.

The System is a decision-support tool. It does not perform drone flight
execution, image stitching, or laboratory-grade chemical analysis, and it
does not replace professional environmental or engineering judgment.
